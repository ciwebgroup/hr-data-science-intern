# CI Web Group Job Fair Strategy
## Data Science Internship Recruitment

---

# Key Considerations for Successful Job Fair Presence

## Pre-Event Preparation

### Booth Design & Layout
- **Theme**: "Shape the Future of Home Services with Data"
- **Color Scheme**: Modern tech palette (deep blues, clean whites, accent orange)
- **Layout**: Open, inviting design with distinct zones:
  - **Welcome/Greeting Area**: Initial engagement point
  - **Interactive Demo Zone**: Live data visualizations and tools
  - **Conversation Corner**: Comfortable seating for in-depth discussions
  - **Take-Away Station**: Branded materials and swag

### Technology Integration
- **Interactive Displays**: 
  - 55" touchscreen showing real-time data dashboards
  - Tablet stations for candidate information capture
  - QR codes linking to application portal and company videos
- **Live Demonstrations**:
  - Real-time geospatial analysis using PostGIS
  - ML model predictions for home services demand
  - Data pipeline visualization with Apache Airflow

### Staffing Strategy
- **Team Composition**:
  - 1 Senior Data Scientist (technical depth)
  - 1 HR Representative (process/benefits)
  - 1 Current Data Science Intern (peer perspective)
  - 1 Engineering Manager (career progression)
- **Shift Schedule**: 2-person minimum at all times, 4-hour rotations

## Engagement Strategies

### Attraction Techniques
- **"Data Challenge" Mini-Games**: 
  - 5-minute predictive modeling challenges
  - Geospatial pattern recognition puzzles
  - Real dataset exploration tasks
- **Interactive Demos**: 
  - "See Your Impact": Show how intern projects directly influence client success
  - "Tech Stack Tour": Hands-on experience with our cutting-edge tools
- **"Day in the Life" Virtual Reality**: Immersive experience of working at CI Web Group

### Conversation Starters
- **Industry-Specific Questions**:
  - "How would you predict HVAC demand spikes during extreme weather?"
  - "What data points would you analyze to optimize a plumber's service routes?"
- **Technical Discussions**:
  - "Tell us about a data project you're proud of"
  - "How would you approach cleaning messy customer data?"
- **Culture Fit Assessment**:
  - "Describe a time you had creative freedom on a project"
  - "How do you handle ambiguous problem statements?"

## Competitive Differentiation

### Unique Value Propositions
1. **Real Impact**: Unlike typical internships, our interns see immediate adoption of their work
2. **Creative Autonomy**: "Create without limits" - unlimited sandbox environment
3. **Cutting-Edge Tech Stack**: Exposure to latest AI/ML tools and infrastructure
4. **Industry Expertise**: Specialized focus on home services - a growing, recession-resistant sector
5. **Mentorship Quality**: Direct access to senior data scientists and domain experts

### Addressing Common Concerns
- **"Is this just grunt work?"**: Showcase actual intern project outcomes and testimonials
- **"Will I learn relevant skills?"**: Demonstrate our comprehensive tech stack alignment with industry standards
- **"What's the career path?"**: Present clear progression from intern → junior → senior → lead roles

## Follow-Up Strategy

### Immediate (Day of Event)
- **Digital Business Cards**: QR codes with LinkedIn connection requests
- **Application Fast-Track**: On-site application submission with expedited review
- **Calendar Booking**: Direct scheduling for follow-up interviews

### Short-term (1-3 days)
- **Personalized Email**: Reference specific conversation points
- **Relevant Content**: Send case studies or blog posts matching their interests
- **Next Steps**: Clear timeline and expectations

### Medium-term (1-2 weeks)
- **Technical Assessment**: Tailored to their background and interests
- **Virtual Office Tour**: Introduce them to the team and environment
- **Project Preview**: Show specific projects they could work on
