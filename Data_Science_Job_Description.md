# Core Responsibilities

## Data Analysis & Insights Generation

### **Targeted Reporting**
- Design and develop custom analytics reports tailored to specific client needs and objectives
- Transform complex datasets into clear, actionable intelligence for business decision-making
- Create automated reporting pipelines that deliver consistent, timely insights to stakeholders

### **Profound Market Insights**
- Uncover hidden patterns and trends within home services market data
- Conduct deep-dive analyses that reveal strategic opportunities and competitive advantages
- Develop predictive models that forecast market shifts and consumer behavior changes

### **Geospatial Analytics**
- Leverage PostGIS and advanced mapping technologies to identify emerging buyer markets
- Optimize service area boundaries and routing strategies using location intelligence
- Create heat maps and geographic visualizations that reveal demand patterns and growth opportunities

## Human Behavior Analysis

### **Consumer Journey Mapping**
- Analyze online search patterns, website interactions, and conversion triggers
- Map customer touchpoints across the entire home services buying journey
- Identify optimization opportunities that improve customer experience and business outcomes

### **Demographic & Psychographic Analysis**
- Expand beyond basic demographics to understand psychological attributes and lifestyle factors
- Develop detailed customer personas that enable precision marketing and service offerings
- Analyze how different audience segments respond to various messaging and channel strategies

### **Campaign Impact Assessment**
- Quantify the effectiveness of marketing channels and campaigns on buyer behavior
- Develop attribution models that accurately measure ROI across complex customer journeys
- Design A/B testing frameworks that optimize marketing performance and customer acquisition
